## Opening hours
Our Cardiff showroom is open Monday to Friday 9am–5:30pm and Saturday 10am–4pm. We are closed on Sundays and bank holidays. Online orders can be placed 24/7.

## Delivery
We deliver across England, Scotland and Wales. Standard delivery takes 3–5 working days and costs £4.95, free on orders over £50. Next-day delivery costs £9.95 if ordered before 2pm. We do not currently deliver to Northern Ireland, the Channel Islands or outside the UK.

## Returns and refunds
You can return unused items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Start a return from your account page or by emailing the support team. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 working days of us receiving the item. Custom-cut items such as blinds made to measure cannot be returned unless faulty.

## Warranty
All furniture comes with a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Electrical items have a 1-year warranty. Damage from misuse or normal wear is not covered.
