# Trade Accounts

Fictional sample content for the demo.

## Who can apply
Trade accounts are for registered businesses such as builders, interior designers and landlords. You need a UK company number or a UTR and a business bank account.

## Trade discount
Trade customers get 15% off list prices, rising to 20% once annual spend passes £10,000. Discounts don't combine with sale prices.

## Payment terms
Approved trade accounts can pay on 30-day invoice terms with a starting credit limit of £2,500. Late payments may pause the account.

## How to apply
Fill in the trade application form on the website. Applications are reviewed within 2 working days.
